The MOON Study · Now recruiting in Atlanta

Help us learn how pregnancy shapes a baby’s growing brain.

The MOON Study follows families from pregnancy through baby’s first year. It is run by the {{ site.title }} at {{ lab.institution }} and funded by the National Institutes of Health.

Email the study team {%- if moon.phone != "" %} or call {{ moon.phone }} {%- endif %}

Contacting us doesn’t enroll you, and taking part is always voluntary. Please don’t include medical details in your first message.

What’s involved

From your first call to baby’s first birthday

  1. Step 1

    A short call

    We explain the study and check whether it’s a good fit for you.

  2. Step 2

    Consent from home

    Go over the consent form with us by phone or video, then sign online.

  3. Step 3 · Pregnancy

    Two MRI visits

    At 24–28 and 32–36 weeks, plus short online food diaries in the second and third trimesters.

  4. Step 4 · 1–2 months

    Baby’s MRI visit

    One MRI visit for your baby in the first two months.

  5. Step 5 · First year

    Check-ins

    Short questionnaires at 6, 9, and 12 months, and play-based activities with your baby at 6 and 12 months.
