Tracing the pathways from maternal health to the developing brain.

Research

Three programs, one pathway.

We combine imaging, machine learning, multi-omics, and longitudinal behavior to build measurable phenotypes and find modifiable pathways for earlier intervention.

01 · Methods

AI for perinatal imaging phenotypes

Learning methods for fetal, placental, newborn, and infant MRI that handle motion, changing anatomy, scanner differences, and limited labels.

How can we recover reliable phenotypes from moving, rapidly changing anatomy?

02 · Mechanisms

Placental and fetal-circulatory pathways

How maternal metabolism, inflammation, stress, medications, and environment shape placental function, fetal hemodynamics, and oxygen delivery.

Where along the maternal–placental–fetal pathway does risk emerge?

03 · Outcomes

From perinatal phenotypes to neurobehavioral risk

Linking imaging phenotypes with regulation, attention, social communication, NICU course, and early psychopathology risk.

Which perinatal phenotypes signal later risk or resilience?
